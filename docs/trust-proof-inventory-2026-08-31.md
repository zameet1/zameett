## Publication rules for the next five case studies

Each case study must have all of the following before publication:

1. A permission record covering the images, project description and any client or brand identity.
2. The brief and exact Zameett role.
3. The deliverables or development stages that can be shown.
4. At least one specific decision or constraint evidenced by project records.
5. An outcome stated no more strongly than its source supports.
6. A confidentiality review with private measurements, supplier terms, prices and files removed or redacted.
7. A canonical case-study URL, links to the relevant service and portfolio pages, and no review or rating schema unless independently verifiable.
