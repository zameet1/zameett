Date: 4 October 2026

Database reviewed: United States, with global volume distribution

Purpose: align high-value keyword families to the correct page without keyword stuffing or search-intent overlap.

The homepage title remains concise because adding every phrase would reduce clarity. The description and visible copy now cover the distinct service entities, and a five-link service directory passes users and crawlers to the most relevant destination.

## Domain and backlink snapshot

SEMrush estimated Authority Score 2, two organic keywords, 160 referring domains and 169 backlinks. The organic-traffic estimate was zero; this is a third-party estimate and must not be treated as GA4 or Search Console data.

The referring-domain sample contained many unrelated, low-authority ranking, casino and automated-link domains. This makes another bulk “quick backlink” campaign more likely to increase risk than useful authority.

## Safe off-page priorities

1. **Build links to a useful resource, not only the homepage.** Promote the existing fashion-development brief/checklist and practical tech-pack guides to fashion incubators, apparel entrepreneurship programmes, textile educators and independent brand resource libraries.
2. **Earn expert citations.** Pitch concise, original contributions on specification quality, sample approval and modest-wear development. Any published claim must stay within verified Zameett capability and use a named author page.
3. **Publish evidence-led case studies.** Once permission and source material are available, create five distinct cases with brief, constraints, deliverables, decision process and approved outcome. These are stronger outreach destinations than generic service copy.
4. **Request genuine partner attribution.** Where Zameett has a real supplier, collaborator or education relationship, ask for an accurate partner/resource mention. Do not create fictional partnerships or reciprocal-link schemes.
5. **Monitor harmful patterns before considering disavowal.** Export backlink data and compare with Search Console. Do not disavow merely because a domain looks weak; act only with evidence of manipulative links, a manual action or a documented risk review.

## Outreach operating rules

- No paid link placements sold primarily for ranking.
- No automated directories, sitewide footer links, private blog networks or keyword-rich guest-post farms.
- No mass exact-match homepage anchors.
- Each prospect must be relevant to fashion design, apparel development, textiles, modest wear or entrepreneurship.
- Prefer branded or descriptive anchors that fit the surrounding sentence.
- Record contact, page relevance, proposed resource, status and live URL so results can be measured.

## Measurement

Track Search Console impressions, average position and clicks by page and query for 28 days before judging the homepage change. Segment the five keyword families and monitor the intended landing page so cannibalisation can be detected early. In SEMrush, compare meaningful referring domains and target-page visibility monthly rather than counting raw backlinks.
